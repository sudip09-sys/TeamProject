From first version to final demo

  1. Agree on the scope: review this version and confirm roles.
  2. Make individual changes: each member builds, tests, and commits an improvement.
  3. Integrate and test: check accounts, shared tasks, conflicts, and mobile layout.
  4. Deploy together: use one backend with persistent storage and public HTTPS.
  5. Rehearse: every member explains and demonstrates their own part.
